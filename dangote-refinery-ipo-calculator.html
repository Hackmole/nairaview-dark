Dangote Refinery IPO calculator: how many shares will your money buy?

In short

At the fixed ₦525 offer price, every ₦525 of your investment buys one share. Enter an amount below — the calculator rounds down to whole shares and shows your leftover.

Type any amount and see instantly how many Dangote Refinery IPO shares it buys at the fixed offer price of ₦525 per share. The minimum subscription is 10 shares (₦5,250).

Excludes bank/platform transaction fees. If the offer is oversubscribed, you may be allotted fewer shares and refunded the difference. Definitive terms are in the offer prospectus.

How the maths works

  • Shares: your amount divided by ₦525, rounded down — you can’t buy fractions of a share.
  • Cost: shares × ₦525. Anything left over stays in your pocket (or covers fees).
  • Minimum: anything below ₦5,250 can’t buy the 10-share minimum.
