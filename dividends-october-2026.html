DIVIDEND DATES · OCTOBER 2026

NGX dividends in October 2026

In short

October is a thin month for NGX dividends — most companies pay between April and September. There is one confirmed payer: PZ Cussons, paying ₦2.50 per share (its first dividend since 2022), with qualification on 9 October and payment on 30 October 2026.

The one to watch: PZ Cussons (PZ)

  • Dividend: ₦2.50 per share, final dividend for the year ended 31 May 2026 — subject to shareholder approval at the AGM.
  • Qualification date: 9 October 2026 (names on the register at close of business).
  • Register closure: 12–16 October 2026.
  • AGM: 28 October 2026, Abuja.
  • Payment date: 30 October 2026 (electronic — an e-dividend mandate is required).
  • Scrip option: shareholders may elect to take shares instead of cash by 23 October 2026 (reference price = 10-day VWAP from 12 Oct).
  • Registrar: First Registrars & Investor Services Limited.

Why it matters

This is PZ Cussons’ first dividend since 2022, and it follows a dramatic turnaround: FY2026 revenue of ₦260.46 billion (+22.5% year-on-year), profit after tax of ₦45.17 billion (+348.7% from ₦10.07 billion), and retained earnings turning positive to ₦4.39 billion from a ₦38.77 billion deficit. The ₦2.50 payout totals roughly ₦9.9 billion to shareholders.
